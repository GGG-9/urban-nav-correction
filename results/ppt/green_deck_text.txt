CHAPTER 04 · SPATIAL INFORMATION
Simulated IMU / GNSS corpus · 48 routes · 8.64 M rows · 8 held-out test routes
Urban navigation correction · Guo Haisu
Each number belongs to a different limit — compute, geometry, and mapping.
48.6×
THROUGHPUT
1 M IMU rows: 2,245 s on CPU vs 46.2 s on one RTX 4090; 177.6× at chunk 500. The full 8.64 M-row corpus filters in 17 min.
90.5 %
ACCURACY
Outage error 104.65 → 9.93 m; worst outage 413 → 28.3 m. The windowed GPU pipeline pays 8.2 % for its parallelism.
0.28 m
MAPPING
Lane-line lateral offset after the prior; 86 % of mapped points inside the 0.5 m HD tolerance.
WHAT WE WOULD TEST NEXT
Replay a real IMU / GNSS log — the corpus here is simulated; the filter and the prior do not change, only the noise model does.
Widen the window — 90 s of burn-in is a requirement, not the window length; a longer window recovers most of the 8.2 %.
Feed the corrector the missing fix — until a sensor supplies what the outage removed, the gate keeps α at zero.
